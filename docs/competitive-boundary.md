# Competitive boundary

Loopgraph interoperates with adjacent categories; it does not replace them feature-for-feature.

| Category | Examples | Their strength | Loopgraph owns |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agent/workflow runtime | LangGraph, Mastra, Temporal | Durable execution, orchestration | Loop contract, policy, escalation semantics |
| Human-in-the-loop | HumanLayer | Approval routing and resume | Exact prepared-action binding in operational cases |
| LLM observability | Langfuse | Traces, evals, datasets | Loop-level evidence, approvals, business outcome |
| No-code automation | n8n, Zapier | Connectors and visual automation | Code-first governed loops and templates |

References: [LangGraph](https://github.com/langchain-ai/langgraph), [Mastra](https://github.com/mastra-ai/mastra), [Langfuse](https://github.com/langfuse/langfuse)
