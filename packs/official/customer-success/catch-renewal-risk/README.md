# Catch Strategic Account and Renewal Risk

This Customer Success App gives Hermes five connected routes: customer health, renewal risk, support escalation, strategic-account escalation, and QBR preparation.

It supports HubSpot + Intercom + Stripe + Slack and Salesforce + Zendesk + billing + Teams. Hermes joins account, contract, support, product adoption, ownership, and incident evidence into one durable problem context. It cannot change commercial terms, promise product work, send customer communication, or update account records without exact approval.

Primary outcomes are risk precision, time to accountable recovery, renewal outcome, support escalation resolution, adoption recovery, QBR preparation time, and review burden.
