# Escalate Strategic Account Risk

This official Loopgraph App packages the original Strategic Account Escalation example as a governed Hermes application.

It joins support, account, renewal, and incident evidence; resolves the affected company; prepares an accountable recovery plan; and separates internal action from customer communication. Hermes creates or updates one durable business problem and asks a human when account identity, impact, or authority is ambiguous.

The pack includes guided risk-policy setup, a Zendesk + Salesforce + Slack recipe, a complete safety suite, and app-level renewal, recovery, response-time, and review-burden outcomes.

The legacy runnable example remains under `examples/strategic-account-escalation` for advanced LoopSpec users. This pack is the marketplace source of truth for new installations.
